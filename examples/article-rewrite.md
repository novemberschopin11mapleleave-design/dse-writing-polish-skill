# Article Rewrite Example

## Original paragraph

```text
School canteen food is very bad. It is expensive and students do not like eating it. The school should ask students what food they want and should put healthy choices on the menu.
```

## Assumed request

- Genre: article for a school newspaper
- Audience: students and school staff
- Tone: constructive and semi-formal
- Preserve all opinions and recommendations

## Rewritten text

```text
The quality of food in the school canteen is a growing concern. Prices are high, and many students find the current choices unappealing. The school could gather student opinions on preferred dishes and ensure that healthier options are included on the menu.
```

## Vocabulary alternatives

| Original | Alternative | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| very bad | a growing concern | More restrained and suitable for a public article |
| expensive | prices are high | Less abrupt, while describing the same issue |
| do not like eating it | find the current choices unappealing | Formal enough for an article; removes personal repetition |
| healthy choices | healthier options | Clear and natural in a menu context |

## Main changes

- Raised the register while keeping the same criticism.
- Removed repetition and added a more article-like topic statement.
- Preserved the recommendation to consult students and improve the menu.
- Did not invent survey results, prices, or evidence.
